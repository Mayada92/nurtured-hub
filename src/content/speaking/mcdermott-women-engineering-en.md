**McDermott International Women in Engineering Day | 2023**

Keynote presentation on career development and work-life balance, sharing personal journey combining engineering career with athletics.

## Presentation Overview

This keynote addressed the challenges and opportunities of maintaining a successful engineering career while pursuing other passions, particularly athletics. The presentation provided practical strategies for work-life integration and career development.

## Key Concepts Covered

- **Work-Life Integration**: Strategies for balancing professional development with personal passions
- **Career Development**: Building a successful engineering career while maintaining multiple interests
- **Personal Journey**: Shared experience as an engineer, athlete, and leader
- **Multiple Passions**: How diverse interests can complement rather than compete with career goals
- **Resilience and Balance**: Maintaining energy and motivation across different life domains

## Audience

- Women engineers at McDermott International
- Engineering professionals interested in work-life balance
- Career development and leadership audiences

## Impact

This presentation demonstrated that:
- Engineering careers can be successfully combined with other passions
- Diverse interests enhance rather than hinder professional growth
- Work-life integration is achievable with the right strategies
- Personal experiences can inspire and guide others

## Why This Matters

This keynote shows leadership in:
- Supporting women in engineering
- Addressing work-life balance challenges
- Sharing practical strategies for career success
- Demonstrating that diverse interests strengthen professional identity
