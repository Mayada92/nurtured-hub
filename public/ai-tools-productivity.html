Research & Creation Tool

Notes to Podcast Transformation

Google's NotebookLM transforms your research notes and documents into engaging, podcast-style audio content. This innovative feature creates natural-sounding conversations between AI hosts who discuss your uploaded content in an accessible format.

Key Features:
  • Converts text documents into conversational podcasts
  • Natural-sounding AI voices with realistic speech patterns
  • Support for multiple languages
  • Customizable conversation format and length
  • One-click export and sharing options

NotebookLM Audio Overview

How It Works:
  1. Upload Content - Add documents, research papers, notes, or any text content to your notebook
  2. Generate Audio Overview - Click to create a podcast-style discussion about your content
