Spoke about positive coexistence with Multiple Sclerosis as part of the Nasya Shifa series organized by Arfa Association for Multiple Sclerosis. Co-presented with Hamad Al-Shaifani, Media Officer at Arfa Association. The talk focused on ways to live positively with MS, sharing insights from personal experience as an engineer at Saudi Aramco and football player at Al-Nahda Club.

## Event Details

- **Event**: Nasya Shifa - Positive Coexistence with Multiple Sclerosis
- **Organization**: Arfa Association for Multiple Sclerosis
- **Date**: December 26, 2023
- **Time**: 7:30 PM
- **Platform**: Zoom
- **Co-Speaker**: Hamad Al-Shaifani, Media Officer at Arfa Association

## Topics Covered

- Positive coexistence strategies with Multiple Sclerosis
- Balancing professional life (engineering) with personal health
- Maintaining active lifestyle (football) while managing MS
- Inspiring others to overcome challenges and pursue their goals

## Impact

This talk demonstrates commitment to supporting the MS community and sharing personal experiences to help others facing similar challenges. The event was part of Arfa Association's mission to "Awaken your hopes to overcome your pain."
