This workshop focused on empowering displaced professionals with practical AI tools and skills to enhance their career prospects and adapt to the evolving job market.

## Audience

- **100+ displaced Palestinian professionals** seeking career development opportunities
- Participants from diverse professional backgrounds looking to enhance their skills
- Individuals navigating career transitions and job market challenges

## Key Concepts Covered

- **AI Tools for Career Development**: Practical applications of ChatGPT, AI-powered resume builders, and job search tools
- **Skill Enhancement**: Using AI for learning, skill development, and professional growth
- **Job Search Strategies**: Leveraging AI tools for networking, application optimization, and interview preparation
- **Hands-On Practice**: Live demonstrations and interactive exercises with AI tools

## Educational Impact

While not children-focused, this workshop demonstrates educational facilitation skills:
- **Adaptive Teaching**: Adapting complex technical content for diverse audiences
- **Practical Application**: Focusing on real-world, immediately applicable skills
- **Empowerment Through Education**: Using education as a tool for career and life advancement
- **Inclusive Learning**: Creating accessible learning experiences for displaced professionals

## Why This Matters

This experience shows ability to:
- Design and deliver educational workshops for adult learners
- Adapt technical content for diverse audiences
- Create engaging, practical learning experiences
- Support career development through education
