**Mawhiba (Saudi Arabia's Gifted Students Program) — Volunteer Instructor (2018)**

Designed and taught petroleum engineering concepts to gifted high school students, adapting complex technical material for age-appropriate learning and creating engaging educational experiences for advanced learners.

## Key Activities

- **Curriculum Design**: Developed age-appropriate lesson plans that introduced complex petroleum engineering concepts in accessible ways
- **Adaptive Teaching**: Adapted technical material (reservoir engineering, production systems, artificial lift) for high school students while maintaining academic rigor
- **Engaging Presentations**: Created interactive learning experiences that challenged gifted students while respecting their developmental stage
- **Mentorship**: Provided guidance and inspiration for students interested in STEM fields, particularly engineering

## Educational Approach

- **Age-Appropriate Content**: Balanced technical accuracy with developmental appropriateness for adolescents
- **Scaffolded Learning**: Built complex concepts from foundational principles
- **Intrinsic Motivation**: Designed activities that sparked curiosity and genuine interest in engineering
- **Real-World Applications**: Connected abstract concepts to practical applications students could understand

## Educational & Developmental Impact

This experience demonstrates:

- **Understanding Adolescent Development**: Applied knowledge of how gifted adolescents learn and process complex information
- **Adaptive Teaching Methods**: Successfully translated advanced technical content for younger learners
- **Educational Psychology in Practice**: Created learning experiences that respect cognitive development while challenging advanced learners
- **Inspiring Future Engineers**: Contributed to students' interest in STEM fields and engineering careers

## Why This Matters

Teaching gifted students requires understanding both the technical subject matter and adolescent cognitive development. This experience shows:
- Ability to adapt complex material for different developmental levels
- Understanding of how gifted learners process information
- Skill in creating engaging, age-appropriate educational experiences
- Commitment to inspiring the next generation of engineers
