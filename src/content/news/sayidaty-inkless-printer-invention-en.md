Featured in Sayidaty magazine for winning first place in Saudi Arabia in the IETpatw (Institution of Engineering and Technology Present Around the World) competition for her innovative inkless printer invention. The invention, patented in 2014, uses laser technology to perform pyrolysis reactions directly on paper surfaces, eliminating the need for ink.

This achievement qualified her for the regional competition in Dubai in July 2017, representing Saudi Arabia in a competition covering Europe, the Middle East, and Africa. As a PhD student in Chemical Engineering at KAUST at the time, this recognition highlighted her contributions to scientific innovation and her role as a pioneering Saudi woman in engineering and research.
