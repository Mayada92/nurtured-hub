**Wasla Connect Workshop | July 2024**

**Live Arabic Workshop for Displaced Palestinian Tech Professionals**

Conducted live 2-hour Arabic workshop for 60+ displaced Palestinian tech professionals on AI tools for career development, job search, and skill enhancement.

## Workshop Overview

This workshop focused on empowering displaced professionals with practical AI tools and skills to enhance their career prospects and adapt to the evolving job market. Delivered entirely in Arabic with bilingual slides.

## Key Activities

- **Live Arabic Workshop**: Delivered interactive 2-hour session in Arabic for displaced Palestinian professionals
- **Practical AI Tools**: Demonstrated ChatGPT, Claude, AI-powered resume builders, and job search optimization tools
- **Hands-On Training**: Provided interactive exercises and live demonstrations with AI tools
- **Career Development Focus**: Covered job search strategies, networking, application optimization, and interview preparation
- **Skill Enhancement**: Taught participants how to use AI for learning, skill development, and professional growth

## Technologies Demonstrated

ChatGPT, Claude, 11Labs, Leonardo.ai, Napkin.ai, Gamma.ai, GenSpark, Udio, Krea.ai

## Impact

- **60+ professionals reached** through live workshop
- **1000+ views** on YouTube recording
- **Empowered participants** with practical AI skills for career advancement
- **Career resilience**: Enabled displaced professionals to leverage AI for job opportunities

## About Wasla Connect

Wasla Connect is a registered 501(c)(3) non-profit organization based in the US. Founded by tech and leadership professionals, Wasla Connect provides free, fully remote mentorship to displaced Palestinians in tech fields, connecting them with experienced professionals for career guidance and skill development.
