Feature article about the training experience in Portugal with Pro Direct Academy. The Saudi player underwent a month-long training and immersion program, training 6 days a week with academy coaches and players, including fitness training and training with a Portuguese women's club (Clube de Albergaria). The experience aimed to improve technical and physical skills to raise the level of women's football in Saudi Arabia.
