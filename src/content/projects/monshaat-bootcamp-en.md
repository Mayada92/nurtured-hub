**AI for Business Development Bootcamp | October 13-15, 2024**

Led 3-day intensive bootcamp on AI applications in business development for entrepreneurs and SME owners in Saudi Arabia's Eastern Province. The bootcamp focused on helping participants understand and implement AI solutions in their businesses through hands-on generative AI projects.

## Bootcamp Overview

The Monshaat AI for Business Bootcamp was designed to introduce entrepreneurs and small business owners to practical AI applications. Over three days, participants learned how to leverage generative AI tools and techniques to enhance their business operations.

## Focus Areas

- **AI Fundamentals in Business**: Introduction to artificial intelligence and machine learning concepts
- **Generative AI for Business Growth**: Practical applications of AI for innovation and business development
- **Emerging AI Topics and Challenges**: Understanding current trends and overcoming implementation barriers
- **Hands-on Projects**: Participants worked on real-world AI applications for their businesses

## Key Topics Covered

- Overview of AI in business
- Resources and requirements by type of use
- Internal use vs product integration approaches
- Practical AI tools and platforms
- Customizing AI solutions for specific business needs

## Impact

The bootcamp provided entrepreneurs with actionable knowledge and tools to integrate AI into their business strategies, fostering innovation and competitive advantage in the digital economy.
