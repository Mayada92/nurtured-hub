**Saudi Aramco — Knowledge Sharing & Professional Development (Additional Role)**

**Oct 2017 – Present | Dhahran, Saudi Arabia**

**Note:** This role runs parallel to my primary engineering positions at Saudi Aramco. While my main responsibilities have included Flow Assurance Engineering, Artificial Lift Engineering, Reservoir Studies, Production Engineering, and Data Science roles, I have simultaneously maintained this additional role in the company's internal education ecosystem for eight years.

For eight years, I have been deeply involved in Saudi Aramco's internal education ecosystem, contributing to the Upstream Professional Development Center (UPDC) and several upstream Communities of Practice. This work is separate from my main engineering duties and focuses exclusively on training, course development, digital capability building, and thought leadership, supporting the professional development of upstream engineers and technical staff across the organization.

## Key Contributions

### Instructor – Upstream Professional Onboarding Program (UPOP) (2024)

Delivered the flagship Digital Transformation in Upstream session as part of the UPOP curriculum, introducing early-career engineers to Aramco's digital ecosystem, emerging technologies, and data-driven decision-making in upstream operations. This session is a core component of the onboarding program for new upstream engineers, providing them with foundational knowledge of digital tools, AI/ML applications, and how technology is transforming upstream operations. The session covers practical examples from real Aramco projects, helping new engineers understand how to leverage digital capabilities in their daily work.

### Content Developer – Unsupervised ML (Anomaly Detection) Course (2025)

Developed the anomaly detection module for UPDC's in-house Unsupervised Machine Learning course. Created comprehensive curriculum materials including lecture slides, hands-on exercises, and practical case studies based on real upstream applications. The module covers key concepts such as isolation forests, autoencoders, and clustering-based anomaly detection, with exercises that use actual production data scenarios. This work supports internal capability building in data science, enabling engineers to identify and address anomalies in equipment performance, production data, and operational systems.

### Lead Instructor – DASCA Certification Program (2025–2026)

Appointed as the exclusive internal instructor for the DASCA (Data Science) certification track, with three full cycles scheduled for 2026. This is a comprehensive professional certification program designed to upskill Aramco engineers in data science fundamentals and advanced techniques.

- **Curriculum Design**: Designing the full 5-day intensive course curriculum covering data science fundamentals, machine learning, statistical analysis, and practical applications in upstream operations
- **Program Delivery**: Delivering the program to multiple cohorts of engineers from various departments, adapting teaching methods to different learning styles and technical backgrounds
- **Adaptive Learning Platform**: Building an adaptive learning platform with an extensive, AI-generated question bank that identifies learner weaknesses, provides personalized study recommendations, and tracks progress to boost exam readiness. The platform uses machine learning to adapt to each learner's pace and focus areas
- **Certification Support**: Providing ongoing support to candidates preparing for the DASCA certification exam, including review sessions, practice exams, and one-on-one coaching

### Internal Thought Leadership & Soft-Skills Training (2017–Present)

Consistently invited to deliver high-impact sessions across the company, reaching hundreds of employees annually. These sessions are requested by various departments and teams, demonstrating recognition of expertise in both technical and soft skills training.

- **AI Ethics (Moral Compass)** – delivered repeatedly across multiple departments, including a company-wide Galaxy Talks broadcast that reached thousands of employees. The session covers ethical considerations in AI development and deployment, responsible AI practices, and real-world case studies from the industry
- **Applied AI & Data Science in Upstream** – technical deep-dive sessions for engineering and digital teams, covering practical ML applications in production optimization, predictive maintenance, and equipment diagnostics. Includes hands-on demonstrations and case studies from actual Aramco projects
- **Innovation Culture Workshops** – supporting the digital organization's innovation initiatives by teaching creative problem-solving, design thinking, and how to foster innovation within technical teams. These workshops help teams develop a culture of experimentation and continuous improvement
- **Teamwork & Workplace Effectiveness** – soft skills sessions requested by various groups, covering effective collaboration, communication strategies, conflict resolution, and building high-performing teams. These sessions are particularly valued by technical teams transitioning to more collaborative work models
- **Early ML Talks** – delivered even at entry level, introducing ML principles to petroleum engineers with no prior data science background. These foundational sessions help engineers understand how ML can enhance their work, making complex concepts accessible through analogies and practical examples

### Subject Matter Expert – Internal Communities of Practice

Recognized SME actively contributing to multiple Communities of Practice, providing technical guidance, reviewing educational materials, and supporting internal capability development initiatives.

- **Petroleum Engineering & Development CoP**: Regular contributor providing expertise on digital transformation in petroleum engineering, reviewing technical content, and presenting on best practices in ML applications for production optimization and equipment diagnostics. Help organize knowledge-sharing sessions and mentor engineers interested in digital solutions
- **AI & Data Science CoP**: Core member contributing to the community's learning objectives, reviewing course materials, and presenting on advanced ML techniques, AI ethics, and responsible AI practices. Support the community's mission to build data science capabilities across upstream departments

These contributions include reviewing curriculum materials, providing technical feedback on course content, presenting on best practices in ML, AI ethics, and digital transformation, and mentoring engineers who are developing their data science skills.

## Scope & Impact

This additional role has reached hundreds of engineers and technical staff across Saudi Aramco's upstream organization over eight years. The work demonstrates extensive experience in:

- **Adult Learning & Pedagogy**: Adapting teaching methods for diverse professional audiences with varying technical backgrounds, from entry-level engineers to senior technical leaders
- **Curriculum Development**: Creating comprehensive educational materials including course outlines, lecture content, hands-on exercises, case studies, and assessment tools
- **Professional Training**: Delivering both technical training (data science, AI/ML, digital transformation) and soft skills development (teamwork, innovation culture, workplace effectiveness)
- **Educational Technology**: Building adaptive learning platforms that use AI to personalize the learning experience and identify areas for improvement
- **Thought Leadership**: Establishing credibility as a subject matter expert through consistent delivery of high-impact sessions and active participation in professional communities

This parallel track of work showcases ability to balance primary engineering responsibilities with additional contributions to organizational learning and development, demonstrating commitment to knowledge sharing and building capabilities across the organization.
