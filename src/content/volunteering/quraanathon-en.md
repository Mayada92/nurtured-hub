**Quraanathon Hackathon (2020)**

**Social Application for Holy Quran**

Developed innovative social application serving the Holy Quran, focusing on reflecting on teachings and enhancing spiritual connection. The app combines technology with meaningful content to enhance users' engagement with religious teachings.

## Project Overview

The Quraanathon hackathon project focused on creating a social application that serves the Holy Quran, with an emphasis on reflection and spiritual connection. The app combines technology with meaningful content to enhance users' engagement with religious teachings.

## Key Features

- **Social Features**: Community engagement tools for users to connect and share
- **Reflection Tools**: Prompts and features designed to encourage thoughtful engagement with teachings
- **Educational Content**: Structured content to support learning and understanding
- **User-Friendly Interface**: Designed for accessibility and ease of use

## Impact

This project demonstrates the application of technology to support spiritual learning and community engagement, showing how digital tools can enhance traditional practices and make religious content more accessible and engaging.
