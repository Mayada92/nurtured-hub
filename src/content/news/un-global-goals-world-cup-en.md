Part of the Greens Saudi Arabia team (@greens_saudi) that won 2nd place at the UN Global Goals World Cup (@ggwcup) in New York, representing Saudi Arabia. The UN Global Goals World Cup is a global tournament that combines football with advocacy for the UN Sustainable Development Goals, bringing together teams from around the world to compete while raising awareness about important global issues.
