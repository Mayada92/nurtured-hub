**AI for Business Development Bootcamp | October 13-15, 2024**

Led 3-day intensive bootcamp on AI applications in business development for entrepreneurs and SME owners in Saudi Arabia's Eastern Province.

## Workshop Structure

- **Day 1:** AI Fundamentals for Business
- **Day 2:** Hands-on Generative AI Projects
- **Day 3:** Implementation & Business Integration

## Topics Covered

- Generative AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, Midjourney)
- Business content creation
- Marketing automation with AI
- Customer service chatbots
- Productivity enhancement

## Guest Speakers

- Mohammed Kaki (FitLife founder) - AI in fitness business
- Nasser Alsharman (Kleen co-founder) - AI in service industry
- Mahmoud Al-Hashem - AI trends & applications

## Participants

- 30+ entrepreneurs & SME owners
- Location: Monshaat Innovation Center, Khobar

## Technologies Taught

ChatGPT, Claude, Midjourney, Leonardo.ai, Runway ML, Synthesia, Jasper AI, Gamma.ai

## Impact

Enabled SMEs to adopt AI for competitive advantage. Participants received official Monshaat certificate of completion.

## Role

Lead Instructor & Curriculum Designer
