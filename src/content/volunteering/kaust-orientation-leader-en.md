**KAUST Student Orientation Leader | 2016 & 2017**

Selected by Graduate Affairs to lead two-week intensive orientation program for new graduate students at King Abdullah University of Science and Technology.

## Key Activities

- **Orientation Program Leadership**: Led comprehensive two-week orientation program for incoming graduate students
- **Student Welcome**: Welcomed and oriented 100+ new graduate students each year (2016 and 2017)
- **Campus Integration**: Organized campus tours, social events, and academic introductions
- **Student Support**: Served as first point of contact for student questions and concerns
- **Cultural Bridge-Building**: Facilitated integration into KAUST community and Saudi culture
- **Logistics Management**: Resolved logistical issues including housing, IT, facilities, and campus resources

## Leadership Skills Developed

- **Event Coordination**: Planned and executed orientation activities and social events
- **Public Speaking**: Delivered presentations and guided campus tours
- **Problem-Solving**: Addressed student concerns and resolved logistical challenges
- **Cultural Bridge-Building**: Helped international students adapt to Saudi culture and KAUST community
- **Student Mentorship**: Provided guidance and support to new students during transition

## Impact

- **200+ students oriented** over two years (100+ per year)
- **Smooth transitions**: Facilitated successful integration of new students into KAUST community
- **Cultural integration**: Supported international students adapting to Saudi culture
- **Community building**: Created welcoming environment for diverse student body

## Why This Matters

This experience demonstrates:
- **Leadership in student services**: Organizing and leading large-scale orientation programs
- **Cross-cultural communication**: Supporting diverse international student population
- **Event management**: Coordinating multiple activities and logistics
- **Student support**: Providing guidance and mentorship to new students
- **Community engagement**: Contributing to positive student experience and campus culture
