Volunteered as a speaker for Arfa Association for Multiple Sclerosis, delivering a talk on "Positive Coexistence with Multiple Sclerosis" as part of their Nasya Shifa series. Co-presented with Hamad Al-Shaifani, Media Officer at Arfa Association, sharing personal experiences as an engineer at Saudi Aramco and football player at Al-Nahda Club living with MS.

## Event Details

- **Event**: Nasya Shifa - Positive Coexistence with Multiple Sclerosis
- **Organization**: Arfa Association for Multiple Sclerosis
- **Date**: December 26, 2023
- **Time**: 7:30 PM
- **Platform**: Zoom (Online)
- **Co-Speaker**: Hamad Al-Shaifani, Media Officer at Arfa Association

## Impact

This volunteer speaking engagement demonstrates commitment to supporting the MS community by sharing personal experiences and strategies for living positively with Multiple Sclerosis. The talk focused on balancing professional life, maintaining an active lifestyle, and inspiring others to overcome challenges and pursue their goals despite health conditions.
