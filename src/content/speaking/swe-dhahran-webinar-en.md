**SWE-Dhahran Webinar | 2021**

"Integrating Engineering Career with Athletics"

Presented strategies for balancing professional development with sports/fitness goals, sharing experience as Eastern Flames FC Vice President and competitive athlete.

## Presentation Overview

This webinar addressed how engineering professionals can successfully integrate athletic pursuits with their careers, using personal experience as a case study.

## Key Concepts Covered

- **Career-Athletics Integration**: How engineering career and athletics can complement each other
- **Time Management**: Strategies for balancing professional responsibilities with training and competition
- **Leadership Skills**: How athletic leadership (Eastern Flames FC VP) enhances professional leadership
- **Resilience**: Building mental and physical resilience through sports
- **Work-Life Balance**: Practical approaches to maintaining multiple commitments

## Audience

- Members of Society of Women Engineers (SWE) - Dhahran Chapter
- Women engineers interested in work-life balance
- Engineering professionals pursuing athletics or fitness goals

## Impact

This presentation showed that:
- Engineering careers and athletics are not mutually exclusive
- Leadership skills from sports translate to professional settings
- Physical fitness and mental resilience support professional performance
- Diverse interests strengthen professional identity

## Why This Matters

This webinar demonstrates:
- Support for women in engineering
- Practical strategies for work-life integration
- Leadership in both professional and athletic domains
- Inspiration for engineers pursuing multiple passions
