Featured in Hia Magazine for achieving global recognition as a PhD student in Chemical Engineering at KAUST. The article highlights her journey from winning first place in Saudi Arabia in the IETpatw competition to qualifying for the international competition covering the Middle East, Europe, and Africa.

## Key Achievements Highlighted

- **First Place in Saudi Arabia**: Won the IETpatw competition with her inkless printer invention (patented in 2014)
- **International Qualification**: Qualified for the regional competition covering Middle East, Europe, and Africa
- **Second Place at KAUST**: Achieved second place at the university level before advancing to the national competition
- **Educational Background**: 
  - Bachelor's in Chemical Engineering from UC Santa Barbara, USA
  - Master's in Mechanical Engineering from KAUST
  - PhD in Chemical Engineering at KAUST (in progress)

## Innovation Journey

The article details how she began patenting her laser-based pyrolysis method for inkless printing during her undergraduate studies in America, supported by the Saudi Cultural Attaché. She then developed the printer concept further in her Master's thesis research at KAUST.

## Future Research

The article mentions her pioneering PhD work on using machine learning to predict chemical reactions—an innovative programming approach where a program learns from input data and becomes capable of predicting other variables. This early work demonstrates her forward-thinking approach to combining engineering with data science and AI.
