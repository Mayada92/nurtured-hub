**Saudi Aramco — Knowledge Sharing & Professional Development**

**Oct 2017 – Present | Dhahran, Saudi Arabia**

For eight years, I have been deeply involved in Saudi Aramco's internal education ecosystem, contributing to the Upstream Professional Development Center (UPDC) and several upstream Communities of Practice. My work focuses on training, course development, digital capability building, and thought leadership, supporting the development of upstream professionals across the company.

## Key Contributions

### Instructor – Upstream Professional Onboarding Program (UPOP) (2024)

Delivered the flagship Digital Transformation in Upstream session as part of the UPOP curriculum, introducing early-career engineers to Aramco's digital ecosystem, emerging technologies, and data-driven decision-making in upstream operations.

### Content Developer – Unsupervised ML (Anomaly Detection) Course (2025)

Developed the anomaly detection module for UPDC's in-house Unsupervised Machine Learning course. Created curriculum materials, exercises, and practical case studies to support internal capability building in data science.

### Lead Instructor – DASCA Certification Program (2025–2026)

Appointed as the exclusive internal instructor for the DASCA (Data Science) certification track, with three full cycles scheduled for 2026.

- Designing the full 5-day course curriculum
- Delivering the program to multiple cohorts
- Building an adaptive learning platform with an extensive, AI-generated question bank to identify learner weaknesses and boost exam readiness

### Internal Thought Leadership & Soft-Skills Training (2017–Present)

Consistently invited to deliver high-impact sessions across the company, including:

- **AI Ethics (Moral Compass)** – delivered repeatedly across departments, including a company-wide Galaxy Talks broadcast
- **Applied AI & Data Science in Upstream** – technical sessions for engineering and digital teams
- **Innovation Culture Workshops** – supporting the digital organization's innovation initiatives
- **Teamwork & Workplace Effectiveness** – soft skills sessions requested by various groups
- **Early ML Talks** – delivered even at entry level, introducing ML principles to petroleum engineers

### Subject Matter Expert – Internal Communities of Practice

Recognized SME contributing to:

- **Petroleum Engineering & Development CoP**
- **AI & Data Science CoP**

Offering expertise, reviewing materials, supporting internal capability development, and presenting on best practices in ML, AI ethics, and digital transformation.

## Educational Impact

This role demonstrates extensive experience in adult learning, curriculum development, and professional training within a large organization. The work spans technical education (data science, AI/ML) and soft skills development, showing ability to adapt teaching methods for diverse professional audiences and create engaging, practical learning experiences that support career development.
