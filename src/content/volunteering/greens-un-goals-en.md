Part of the Greens Saudi Arabia team supporting UN Sustainable Development Goals through football. The team combines athletic excellence with social impact, participating in international tournaments and forums to advocate for global goals and raise awareness about important social and environmental issues.

## Key Achievements

- Won 2nd place at UN Global Goals World Cup in New York (2020)
- Represented Saudi Arabia in international tournaments
- Participated in MISK Youth Forum panel in New York (2019)
- Advocated for UN Sustainable Development Goals through football

## Impact

The Greens Saudi Arabia team uses the power of sport to advocate for the UN Sustainable Development Goals, demonstrating how football can be a platform for positive social change and global awareness.
