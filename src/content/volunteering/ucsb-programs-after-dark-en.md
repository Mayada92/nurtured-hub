**UC Santa Barbara - Programs After Dark Manager | November 2014 - June 2015**

Paid student position organizing weekly educational and social programs for university apartment residents at UC Santa Barbara.

## Key Activities

- **Weekly Program Planning**: Planned and executed weekly educational and social programs for university apartment residents
- **Budget Management**: Managed budget and logistics for events and programs
- **Collaboration**: Worked with campus departments and external vendors to organize events
- **Marketing & Outreach**: Promoted programs through social media and campus outreach initiatives
- **Event Execution**: Coordinated logistics and ensured successful program delivery

## Program Types

- **Study Skills Workshops**: Educational programs supporting academic success
- **Wellness Events**: Health and wellness focused activities for residents
- **Cultural Celebrations**: Events celebrating diverse cultures and communities
- **Career Panels**: Professional development and career exploration opportunities

## Leadership Skills Developed

- **Program Development**: Designed and implemented diverse programming for residential community
- **Budget Management**: Managed financial resources for events and activities
- **Marketing & Communication**: Promoted programs through multiple channels
- **Collaboration**: Worked with various campus departments and external partners
- **Event Management**: Coordinated logistics and execution of multiple programs

## Impact

- **Weekly programming**: Provided consistent educational and social opportunities for residents
- **Community building**: Fostered connections among university apartment residents
- **Student engagement**: Increased participation in campus activities
- **Early leadership**: Demonstrated organizational and leadership skills in student role

## Why This Matters

This early leadership experience shows:
- **Program development**: Ability to design and implement diverse programming
- **Resource management**: Managing budgets and logistics effectively
- **Community engagement**: Creating opportunities for student connection and growth
- **Professional skills**: Early development of event management and marketing skills
- **Student leadership**: Contributing to positive campus experience for peers
